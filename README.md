## Features:

1. Live camera:
   Your webcam but dw it asks for your consent first!
2. Privacy:
   Your pictures are not stored! upon clicking refresh they clear up
3. Date feature:
   Personally my fav feature, it writes the date and time of capture at bottom giving it the polaroid feel.
4.Animations:
   The photos are randomly rotated.
5.Unlimited photos:
  You can take unlimited photos.   
      
   
   

## Limitations
Camera access must be explicitly granted by the user each session (unless permissions are remembered by the browser).

Some mobile browsers may default to the front-facing camera; switching cameras cant be done.

No storage thus refreshing the page clears all captured polaroids.

Camera access requires a secure context — either https:// or localhost. It will not work over plain http:// on most modern browsers.
